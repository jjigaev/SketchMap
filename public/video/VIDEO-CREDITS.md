# Hero motion study credits

## Current hero — Almaty Botanical Garden

`forma-almaty-botanical-12s.mp4`, `forma-almaty-botanical-poster.jpg`, `forma-almaty-botanical-portrait-12s.mp4`, and `forma-almaty-botanical-portrait-poster.jpg` are adapted from [**Алматы, ботсад сверху.jpg** by Nikolai Bulykin](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:%D0%90%D0%BB%D0%BC%D0%B0%D1%82%D1%8B,_%D0%B1%D0%BE%D1%82%D1%81%D0%B0%D0%B4_%D1%81%D0%B2%D0%B5%D1%80%D1%85%D1%83.jpg), a 4891 × 3665 aerial photograph of the real Almaty Botanical Garden. The source was downloaded on 2026-09-26.

The image was cropped separately for landscape and portrait compositions, gently color adjusted, and animated with a subtle 12-second reversible camera move. It is a motion study made from one photograph, **not camera-recorded video footage**. The posters were rendered separately at higher resolution using the opening-frame compositions. All four adapted media files are offered under the [Creative Commons Attribution–ShareAlike 4.0 International license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Credit: **Nikolai Bulykin / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0; adaptation by FORMA demo project**. This credit does not imply the photographer endorses the site or that FORMA designed the garden.

The landscape MP4 is a silent 12-second H.264 loop at 1600 × 900 and 24 fps (10.6 MB); its poster is 1920 × 1080 (1.2 MB). The portrait MP4 is a silent 12-second H.264 loop at 720 × 960 and 24 fps (5.6 MB); its poster is 900 × 1200 (0.6 MB). Both versions are hosted locally for static deployment. Motion-sensitive and Save-Data visitors can use a poster without loading the animation.
